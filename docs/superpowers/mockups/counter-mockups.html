Counter mechanic — 4 controller-friendly options

Same deal everywhere: Vorn wants 8 rare, offering 84/cap. Hidden truth (so you can feel the bands): he'll take up to ~102, counter-back up to ~127, and refuses beyond that. Every option resolves with the real rules — push each one into all three zones. Keyboard arrows + Enter work everywhere (that's the d-pad/A-button simulation) — click a phone first to focus it. RESET in the corner re-arms the deal.

1 — THREE PUSHES

No number entry at all. Three fixed pushes (safe / firm / greedy) computed off their offer. One tap = sent. The risk ladder IS the interface. D-pad: left/right + A.

2 — SLIDER

Drag (or d-pad nudge) along a green→red risk track, big live number, then SEND. Full precision, risk visible as position + color.

3 — HOLD TO PUSH

Hold one big button (hold A on pad, Space on keyboard) and the ask climbs with acceleration — release to stop, send it, or keep pushing. Tactile greed: the longer you hold, the greedier you are.

4 — BIG STEPPER

Two giant arrows (±2/cap, hold to repeat; LB/RB = ±10), huge center number, risk in words. The classic, at controller size.
